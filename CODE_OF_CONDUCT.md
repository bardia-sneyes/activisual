# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Activisual welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others learn.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing sensitive trace data.
- Do not harass, intimidate, discriminate against, or personally attack others.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer at [sneyes69@gmail.com](mailto:sneyes69@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe, constructive project community.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
